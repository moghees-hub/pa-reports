Lead Gen’s leads are more serious. They turn up, they buy, and they buy bigger plans.

Count the whole deal

Not just the first payment

Right now each new client only counts as their first payment. A client who pays 6 months upfront is counted in full. A monthly client only counts as one month, even though most stay for several.

AI signed more monthly clients (28 of 37) than Lead Gen (13 of 24), so AI’s money is undercounted more than Lead Gen’s. If we assume every monthly client stays 6 months, the same as an upfront deal:

Lead Gen still makes more than double per booked call. AI’s weekly total pulls further ahead because of its volume.

Simple fix going forward: value monthly clients at their monthly fee times the average number of months clients actually stay.

The season

January is our busiest time of year

AI ran from January to March, the New Year rush, when more people than usual want to get in shape. That pushed its number of bookings up. Lead Gen ran over the summer, outside that peak. So part of the gap in volume is the time of year, not just the channel.

Next steps

To keep improving Lead Gen

  • Book more calls, keep the quality.Volume is the only thing holding Lead Gen back.
  • Mark every call.Closers should mark each call as showed or no-show so the numbers stay accurate.
  • Re-run on 18 October.Lead Gen will then have a full 90 days, with more October calls marked.

Caveat. Some calls were never marked as showed or no-show (27% for AI, 19% for Lead Gen), so the turn-up rates use the calls that were marked. Sales use the lead source on the Sales Dashboard. Most match a call on that channel’s calendar (32 of 37 for AI, 20 of 24 for Lead Gen).

How this was worked out. Calls come from the AI calendar (“Consultation Call Booking”) and the Lead Gen calendar (“Physique Academy Consultation”) in GoHighLevel. Sales and first payments come from the Sales Dashboard, converted to £. “Turned up” is showed out of showed plus no-show. “Bought” is new clients out of calls that showed.
